Workspace map: ../CLAUDE.md. Backend (game server) lives at `../Kayak-and-surf -backend` (note the space); the only link to it is `src/net.js` (contract: see the backend CLAUDE.md).

- Surfing down the silver lane: `src/surf.js` `createSurf()` (config `SURF`). The silver lane can't be entered from the pool: slide.js's slide pusher walls off its foot (and pushes anyone inside it out into the blue lane); the gold arrow on its foot points downhill and is decoration only. The only way in is `surf.surfDown()` (wired to the HUD's Surf! button via `hud.on('surf')`, shown in blue-lane ride mode), which starts the ride mid-slide from wherever the raft is: it hops sideways over the rails into the silver lane (`SURF.shiftHop`) while spinning round (`turnTime`) to face downhill as the player stands into `ANIM.surf` (side-on, knees bent, arms out; crouch with speed, bank into carves, chatter), surfs down (slope physics: gravity, friction, quadratic drag; W tuck / S brake; A/D carve, rails knock it back; `fullSpeed` scales the effects), then slams onto the flat and skids to rest within `stopDistance` (< 5 m); from km up the descent is warped (`st.warp`: metres covered per metre of speed) so no ride takes much over `longRide` (30 s), the effects keeping the real speed, sits down and paddles on. - Paddling up the slide: `src/climb.js` `createClimb()` (config `CLIMB`, `ANIM.climb`). Off the surf, `climb.pose` (pitch/roll onto the slope, eased in at the foot; each stroke rolls the raft toward the paddle, swings the bow away (`pose.yaw`, added to the group's yaw), lifts the nose and heaves it) is what main.js applies to the player group; `update()` runs after stamina.js and pulses `player.moveSpeed` per stroke (lunge on the pull, sag on the recovery), feeds the animator `climb: { effort, tired }` (lean into the slope + look up with effort; tired below `CLIMB.tiredBelow` stamina: rounded back, head drooping between strokes, shorter strokes, faster/deeper breathing with heaving shoulders) and triggers the sounds; `effects()` throws the bow wave and the paddle-catch splash into spray.js. player.js keeps an auto-climbing raft on `slideSurfaceAt` so a long frame can't drop it through the ramp. Steering while climbing (A/D, player.js): the bow turns up to `STAMINA.steerAngle` toward the view's left/right (`player.climbTurn`, eased by `steerEase`) and the raft goes where it points; the blue lane's rails clamp it (`raftHalfWidth`), knock it back (`railBounce`), straighten the bow and set `player.climbBump` (one knock per hit; held against a rail it runs along it). climb.js banks the raft into the turn (`CLIMB.bank`), plays the knock (sfx.bump, camera shake, water slapped up that side), piles the bow wave on the side it crabs into, and passes `steer` to the animator (left: wide forward sweep, right: deep draw; trunk leans/twists and the head looks into the turn, `ANIM.climb.sweep*`/`draw*`/`steer*`) and to `sfx.paddle(power, pull, turn)` (sweep swoosh / draw gulp and slap). Sounds (sfx.js climb section, panned via `near()` with a little reverb): `paddle(power, pull)` (catch plunk + splash, dragged-water swirl, exit sheet + drips), `creak()` (lashings, stick-slip squeak), `breath('out'|'in', tired)` (exhale on each catch once tiring, inhale on the recovery, panting when spent and stopped), `setClimb(level, pace, dt)` loop (lane water gurgling and slapping the bow).
